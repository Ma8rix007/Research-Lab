Research Lab

Research Lab is a personal tool used by a single person. Once a week it summarises public short-form video trends and writes them into one Google Doc that its owner uses for content planning. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Privacy policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

What it accesses. When its owner signs in with Google, Research Lab asks for two permissions:

  • Google Sheets (read only): to read the owner's own content-tracking spreadsheet.
  • Google Docs: to replace the text of one specific document that the owner chose.

How the data is used. The data is used only to produce that weekly summary. Research Lab does not read any other files, and it does not change the spreadsheet.

Storage and sharing. The sign-in token and all working data stay on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or model training.

Limited Use. Research Lab's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access. Access can be withdrawn at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
